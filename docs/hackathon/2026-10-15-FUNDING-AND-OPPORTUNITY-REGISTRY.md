# Funding & Opportunity Registry — 2026-10-15

## Purpose
Keep Shellfish / XRPL hackathon work focused while tracking grants, startup credits, agent OPEX credits, hackathons, accelerators, bounties, and genuine sources of capital without confusing them with XRPL lending liquidity.

## Rule: do not mix funding types

### 1. Lending liquidity / capital
Assets that can actually be deployed into XRPL lending positions or vaults, such as XRP, RLUSD, issued assets, investor capital, grants paid as usable cash/stablecoins, or later protocol liquidity partners.

### 2. Agent OPEX / tool credits
Credits that reduce the cost of research, APIs, browser tools, AI inference, cloud hosting, CI/CD, security, and data. These preserve lending capital but are not themselves vault liquidity unless explicitly redeemable/transferrable.

### 3. Ecosystem / growth programs
Hackathons, accelerators, residencies, builder grants, technical support, investor introductions, and visibility programs that may lead to grants or capital.

## Funding strategy for Shellfish

### Spend credits on operations, preserve capital for finance
Use cloud/tool credits for:
- AI inference and agent orchestration
- web/search/data acquisition
- CI/CD and security scanning
- telemetry, storage, logs, replay evidence
- API gateway / edge protection
- blockchain RPC/streams

Keep actual lending capital separate for:
- Devnet/Testnet demonstration assets
- later RLUSD/XRP vault liquidity
- real partner/investor capital only after legal/security review

## Do not let funding programs drive architecture
A program is useful only when it subsidizes something already justified by the architecture. No migration, new provider, token, bridge, or dependency should be added solely because credits are available.
